About John Kelly

Experienced legal counsel serving Sonoma, California since 2003, with a focus on trust and estate matters, litigation, and business planning.

Professional Background

John Kelly has been practicing law in Sonoma, California since establishing his private practice in 2003. With a strong educational foundation from UC Berkeley School of Law and UCLA, John brings over two decades of legal experience to every client matter.

His practice focuses on trust and estate disputes, comprehensive estate planning, civil litigation, and business planning. This focused approach allows him to provide deep expertise in these critical areas of law while maintaining the personalized service that clients value.

Whether representing clients in complex trust litigation, guiding families through estate planning, or advising business owners on strategic legal matters, John is committed to providing clear counsel and effective advocacy.
